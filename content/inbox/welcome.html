How these notes work

22 November 2026 2 min read inbox

In short

Every page is one research note: a short summary, a table of contents, numbered citations, and a plain stylesheet. Notes are generated from a question and published as static files.

Contents
  1. Layout
  2. Citations

Layout

A note opens with a summary box, then a contents list, then sections with headings. The body text is capped at roughly seventy characters per line so it stays readable on a phone and on a wide monitor.

Category paths mirror a folder tree, so hobby/cubing/f2l produces a page two levels deep with its own index page above it.

Citations

Claims carry bracketed numbers that resolve to the source list at the bottom of the page.[1] A source that is not cited is not listed, and a citation without a source is a bug.

If a claim cannot be traced to a source, it belongs in a draft, not in a published note.
